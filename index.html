04

GhostB

Full-Stack / Machine Learning · Feb 2025 – Mar 2025

A budgeting app that targets psychology, not just numbers. Machine learning classifies each expense, and unnecessary purchases are inflated by up to 60%, recreating the friction of spending real cash.

PythonFastAPISQLAlchemyscikit-learnJavaScriptHTMLCSS

Click to see engineering details

04 — Details

Problem

  • Digital money doesn't hurt to spend. Balances are just numbers on a screen, so spending lacks the psychological friction of handing over cash, and by the end of the month it's hard to say where the paycheck went.

Solution

  • GhostB brings that awareness back. When a user logs an expense, a Random Forest classifier, pre-trained and then tuned on that user's own transaction history, labels it as needed or unnecessary. Unnecessary charges are inflated by up to 60%, so careless spending is felt immediately, and the inflated difference is banked as a ghost budget the user can claim back or allocate to savings.

Engineering Highlights

  • Full-stack financial management web application
  • User-specific training layered on a pre-trained Random Forest classifier
  • Real-time expense classification: needed vs. unnecessary
  • Cognitive spending mechanism inflating unnecessary charges up to 60%
  • Ghost budget system for claiming back or saving the difference
  • Backend APIs and database models for financial data
